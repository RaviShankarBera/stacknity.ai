Privacy Policy

How Stacknity.ai collects, uses and protects personal data - on this website and in client engagements.

LAST UPDATED: 28 SEPTEMBER 2026  ·  APPLIES TO: stacknity.ai website, client accounts and Stacknity.ai services

1. Who we are

Stacknity.ai is an AI consulting venture of Stacknity Technologies, based in Bengaluru, India ("Stacknity.ai", "we", "us"). For the purposes of applicable Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000, we are the data fiduciary for personal data collected through this website and the data processor (or fiduciary, as applicable) for data handled under client engagements.

You can reach us at stacknity.technologies@gmail.com for any privacy question, request or complaint.

2. What we collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Contact details you submit through our enquiry form or by email - your name, company, work email and the message you send.
  • Account details when you create a client account - your name, email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Project information you share during an engagement - documents, workflow descriptions and business data needed to deliver the work, under the terms of that engagement.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic technical data when you browse the site - browser type, device, approximate region and pages visited. The site is served via GitHub Pages, whose operator may log standard server data under its own privacy practices.
  • Authentication session data from our auth provider, used only to keep you signed in.

We do not run advertising trackers, sell personal data, or profile visitors for marketing.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about a potential or active engagement.
  • To create and operate your client account and dashboard.
  • To deliver contracted services, which may include processing your business data to design, build or run AI workflows on your behalf.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations under Indian law.

4. Client data in AI work

When an engagement involves your data (documents, records, customer data):

  • We use it only to deliver that engagement, under the confidentiality terms of our agreement with you.
  • We do not use one client's data to train or fine-tune models for another client.
  • Where third-party AI providers are used, we select them under the engagement's requirements and their processing is governed by their data terms plus our agreement with you.
  • Client work is confidential by default. We never name clients or reuse their data in marketing without written permission.

5. Where data is processed

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and authentication is provided by Supabase - both may process data outside India. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we do so in compliance with applicable law, including any conditions under the DPDP Act.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiry emails and form submissions: up to 24 months, or longer if they lead to an engagement. Account data: until you delete your account or ask us to. Engagement data: for the period agreed in the engagement, then returned or deleted as agreed. Legal and accounting records: as long as Indian law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to access your personal data, correct or erase it, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. To exercise any of these, email stacknity.technologies@gmail.com. We respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the timelines required by law. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access limited to the people delivering your work, and human review built into automated systems. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it on this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Stacknity.ai (a Stacknity Technologies venture), Bengaluru, India. Email: stacknity.technologies@gmail.com.
