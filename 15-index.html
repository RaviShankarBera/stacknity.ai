01

Map the workflow

We sit with your team and chart how work actually moves: the handoffs, the repetitive steps, the places things stall. The real problem is usually hiding in one of them.

02

Choose the right AI

Only the steps where AI clearly helps get automated. The right model for the job, at a cost that makes sense for your size, not the loudest tool on the market.

03

Build, measure, improve

We ship the automation, watch the numbers, and keep tuning. If it is not saving your team time or money, we change it until it does.
