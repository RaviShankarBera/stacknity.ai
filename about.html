WHO IS BEHIND IT

Built by a builder.

Stacknity.ai is the AI consulting venture of Stacknity Technologies - the same build-first mindset, pointed at the question every company is now asking: where does AI actually help us?

Ravi Shankar Bera has been building for the web since 2018. He founded Stacknity Technologies, a Bengaluru agency that ships websites, mobile apps and digital marketing for growing teams, and he still runs it as Founder & CEO.

He holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and an LLB, an uncommon pairing that shapes how Stacknity.ai works: engineer the system properly, and be honest about what it should and should not do.

Stacknity.ai is the next step. Eight years of shipping software for real businesses taught a simple lesson: technology only pays when it fixes a problem someone actually has. That is why every Stacknity.ai engagement starts with the workflow, not the tool.

Today the venture focuses on four things: AI consulting for day-to-day business problems, low-cost LLM training and fine-tuning, agentic AI systems built on LangGraph and LangChain, and AI engineers, RAG specialists and FDEs deployed per project.

WHAT WE STAND BY

How we work,
written down.

01

Problem before platform

If a step does not need AI, we say so - even when it costs us the sale. The audit is honest because the build only works when it is.

02

A human stays accountable

Every system we ship keeps a person in charge of the final call on anything that matters: money, customers, compliance, communication.

03

Measured, not promised

We agree the numbers before we build - hours saved, errors cut, days shortened - then report against them. If it is not working, we change it until it does.
