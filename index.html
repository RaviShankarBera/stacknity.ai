Well-documented, everyday business problems across industries - the kind Stacknity.ai exists to fix. For each one: how we map the workflow and where AI actually earns its place. Client work stays confidential; the approach doesn't.

01CONFIDENTIAL · LOGISTICS

Paper proof-of-delivery slows billing

Delivery notes and lorry receipts arrive as WhatsApp photos and email attachments, while invoicing waits for someone to type them up.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Audit the delivery-to-invoice workflow first
  • Extraction pipeline reads PODs and validates them against orders
  • Exceptions go to a human; clean documents bill the same day
02CONFIDENTIAL · HEALTHCARE

Prior authorizations eat clinical hours

Staff re-read payer policy documents and re-enter the same patient details for every authorization request.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • RAG over payer policies and patient records
  • Draft authorization packets with citations to the exact policy clause
  • A clinician approves every submission before it leaves
03CONFIDENTIAL · E-COMMERCE

Support tickets outgrow the team

Order status, returns and refund questions bury the support team, and response time slips as volume grows.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Classify and route every incoming ticket
  • RAG answers grounded in store policies and live order data
  • Humans keep refunds, disputes and edge cases
04CONFIDENTIAL · MANUFACTURING

Downtime reports written from memory

Maintenance and quality data lives in logbooks and spreadsheets, so root-cause analysis starts from zero after every breakdown.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Structure machine, shift and inspection data in one place
  • Agents draft shift and root-cause reports straight from the logs
  • An engineer signs off; the history stays searchable forever
05CONFIDENTIAL · FINANCE

Invoice matching is a full-time job

Three-way matching across purchase orders, receipts and invoices is manual, and month-end close drags for days.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Extraction fine-tuned on the company's invoice formats
  • A matching agent reconciles PO, receipt and invoice line by line
  • Only mismatches reach a human reviewer
06CONFIDENTIAL · INSURANCE

Claims intake is copy-paste work

First-notice documents, photos and forms get re-keyed into the claims system before triage can even start.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Intake agents read submissions and extract the entities that matter
  • Score and route each claim to the right adjuster
  • A full audit trail on every automated step
07CONFIDENTIAL · LEGAL

Contracts reviewed line by line

Every NDA and vendor agreement needs the same clause checks, and renewal dates get missed in shared drives.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Extraction fine-tuned for the firm's clause patterns
  • RAG answers with citations to the exact clause text
  • The reviewer decides; the system tracks every date
08CONFIDENTIAL · SAAS

Regression suites grow faster than QA

Every release adds more tests, failures take hours to triage, and flaky tests quietly erode trust in the suite.

HOW STACKNITY SOLVES IT
  • Agents generate and maintain test cases from real user flows

  • QA reviews a short diagnosis instead of raw logs
09CONFIDENTIAL · REAL ESTATE
