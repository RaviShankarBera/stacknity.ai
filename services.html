WHAT WE DO

Four services.
One goal: work that moves.

Every engagement starts the same way: we map the workflow, find where AI genuinely helps, then build the smallest system that proves it. These are the four shapes that work takes.

CONSULTING

AI Consulting

We consult on the day-to-day problems slowing your team: find the real problem, decide where AI fits, design the workflow, then automate the process. Advice you can act on, not a slide deck.

  • Workflow audit of day-to-day operations
  • AI fit analysis per step
  • Automation design and rollout
HOW IT RUNS

A short discovery on your real workflow, a written map of where AI pays off and where it does not, then a build plan with costs and expected savings. You approve each step before it starts.

CUSTOM MODELS

LLM Training & Fine-Tuning

Custom-trained and fine-tuned language models at low cost, so the AI speaks your business: your documents, your tone, your edge cases, instead of generic answers from the internet.

  • Low-cost training runs
  • Fine-tuned on your data
  • Evaluated before it ships
HOW IT RUNS

We prepare your data, run small fine-tunes on open models, and score them against real examples from your business before anything ships. You keep the model and the evaluation set.

AGENTIC SYSTEMS

Multi-Agent AI

Agentic AI systems built on LangGraph and LangChain: multiple agents that plan, act and hand work to each other across your tools, with a human in charge of the final call.

  • LangGraph and LangChain builds
  • Agents wired into your tools
  • Human-in-the-loop by design
HOW IT RUNS

We design the agent graph around your workflow, wire it into the tools your team already uses, and put approval steps where judgment matters. Every automated action leaves an audit trail.

TALENT ON DEMAND

AI Engineers, Deployed

AI engineers, RAG specialists and forward-deployed engineers (FDEs), placed per your project requirements. The right specialist inside your team, for exactly as long as you need them.

  • AI engineers per project needs
  • RAG specialists
  • Forward-deployed engineers
HOW IT RUNS

Tell us the project and the gaps. We match an engineer or specialist to the requirement, they embed with your team, and the engagement scales up or down with the work - project basis, no bench.
